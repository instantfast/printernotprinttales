Canon Printer Not Printing: Troubleshooting for Windows and Mac
==============================================================

Is your Canon printer not printing even though it is switched on? Documents may remain in the queue, the printer may appear offline, or paper may come out blank. Each symptom points to a different problem.

Start by checking the printer display, paper supply, selected printer, and connection. These checks can help you identify the cause before changing drivers or running maintenance.

This guide explains what to check when your Canon printer won't print from a computer, laptop, or phone over WiFi or USB.

Why Is My Canon Printer Not Printing?
-------------------------------------

A printing problem can begin at the printer, in the connection, or inside the application sending the document.

Common causes include:

* An empty paper tray or incorrectly loaded paper.
* A paper jam, open cover, or displayed error.
* An incorrect printer selected in the application.
* A disconnected USB cable or wireless connection.
* Paused printing or a blocked print queue.
* An outdated or incompatible printer driver.
* An empty ink cartridge or ink tank.
* Blocked printhead nozzles.
* Incorrect paper size or media settings.

Notice what happens when you send a document. No response, a queued job, and a blank printed page require different troubleshooting steps.

Check the Printer Before Changing Settings
-------------------------------------------

Make sure the printer is powered on and has finished starting. Wake it if it is asleep.

Check its screen or indicator lights. If an error appears, record the complete message or code. Resolve visible problems such as a paper jam or open cover before sending more documents.

Load suitable paper and adjust the guides so they hold the stack without bending it. Confirm that the selected paper size matches the paper in the tray.

If your model has several paper sources, check that the printing settings point to the tray containing paper.

Try a Printer Test Page
-----------------------

Use the printer's maintenance menu to print a nozzle check or another built-in test page, where available. The process varies by model.

If the printer produces its own test page, focus next on the computer, connection, or application.

If the printer cannot print a built-in page, investigate the displayed error, paper handling, or ink system first. Reinstalling computer software is unlikely to resolve a problem that also affects printing directly from the printer.

Canon Printer Won't Print from Computer
---------------------------------------

Select the Correct Printer
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Open the print dialog in your application and check the selected device.

Choose the Canon printer connected to your computer. Avoid selecting a virtual PDF printer or an old printer entry.

If several Canon entries appear, choose the one that matches your current USB or network connection.

Check the Print Queue
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Open the printer queue and look for failed or stuck documents. Cancel unwanted jobs and wait for them to clear before sending another file.

Resume printing if the queue is paused. If a Use Printer Offline setting is available and enabled, turn it off.

Cancelling a document removes it from the queue, so you will need to send it again.

Try a Different Document
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Print a short text document from another application. If it works, the original file or application may be causing the problem.

For a PDF, try another PDF viewer. Check the preview, paper size, and page range before printing again.

Canon Printer Not Printing on Windows 11
----------------------------------------

Open Settings, select Bluetooth & devices, and choose Printers & scanners.

Select your Canon printer and open its queue. Check the status, clear failed jobs, and confirm that the printer is available.

Restart the printer and computer, then try a test page.

If the printer remains unavailable, check its connection before removing and adding it again. Use a driver intended for your exact model and Windows version.

Canon Printer Won't Print on Windows 10
---------------------------------------

Open Settings, select Devices, and choose Printers & scanners.

Choose your Canon printer, inspect its queue, and remove failed documents. Confirm that your application is sending the job to the correct printer.

If the queue remains stuck, restarting the Windows Print Spooler may help. Open the Services application, find Print Spooler, and choose Restart.

This temporarily affects print processing on that computer. After it restarts, check the queue and send one small test document.

Canon Printer Not Printing on Mac
---------------------------------

Open your Mac's printer settings and select the Canon printer. Open its queue and resume printing if paused.

Remove failed documents and check that the printer is connected through the intended USB or wireless connection.

If the printer works from another device but remains unavailable on the Mac, remove its existing entry and add it again.

Choose AirPrint when available for your model, or select compatible printer software. Confirm the printer selection again in the application before printing.

Canon Printer Connected to WiFi but Not Printing
------------------------------------------------

A WiFi connection does not guarantee that your computer can communicate with the printer.

Check the Network Name
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Confirm that the printer and computer are on the same local network. Guest networks or device isolation settings may prevent communication.

The devices can use different wireless bands if your router allows communication between them.

Reconnect After Network Changes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If you changed your router, WiFi name, or password, reconnect the printer using its wireless setup process.

Once connected, check whether the computer still uses an old printer entry or network address. Adding the printer again may refresh outdated settings.

Check the Wireless Signal
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Move the printer closer to the router if its signal is weak. An unstable connection can delay printing or interrupt larger jobs.

If every connected device fails to print, investigate the printer and network. If only one computer fails, focus on that computer's settings.

Canon Printer Not Printing Through USB
--------------------------------------

Reconnect the USB cable securely at both ends. Connect directly to the computer while testing rather than through a hub or docking station.

Try another USB port and, if available, another compatible cable.

Wait for the computer to recognize the printer, then choose the USB printer entry. An existing wireless entry may remain offline even while a cable is attached.

Canon Printer Printing Blank Pages
----------------------------------

If paper moves through the printer but comes out blank, first check that the document contains visible content and that you selected the correct pages.

Review the ink levels or cartridge status using the method available on your model. For a recently installed cartridge, check that any required protective packaging was removed and that the cartridge is seated correctly.

Print a nozzle check pattern. Missing lines or absent colours can indicate an ink delivery or nozzle problem.

Run cleaning only as directed for your model, then check the pattern again. Repeated cleaning uses ink and should not be continued indefinitely.

Do not touch electrical contacts or attempt to remove a printhead unless your model's instructions describe that procedure.

Canon Printer Not Printing Black or Colour
------------------------------------------

If your Canon printer is not printing black, check the black ink supply and nozzle check pattern.

If colour is missing, review the colour ink levels and confirm that the application is not set to grayscale.

Match the paper type setting to the paper loaded. Unsuitable media settings can affect print quality, although they do not explain every missing-colour problem.

If the nozzle check looks correct but one document prints incorrectly, review that application's colour and quality settings.

Canon Printer Not Responding
----------------------------

A Canon printer not responding message usually requires checking communication and device selection.

Restart the printer and computer, verify the connection, and select the correct printer entry.

If the printer can produce its own test page but cannot receive documents, review the driver installation. Use software intended for the exact model and operating system.

For network printing, check whether a VPN or security application restricts local device access. Adjust the relevant setting instead of disabling protection broadly.

Canon Printer Won't Print from a Phone
--------------------------------------

Confirm that your phone and printer are connected to the same local network.

Check that your printer works with the printing method you are using. Available options vary by model and mobile operating system.

Select the correct printer in the application's print menu. If it does not appear, check the printer's wireless status and whether the network allows devices to discover each other.

Try a small document or image before sending a large file.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

Why is my Canon printer not printing even though it has ink?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Ink is only one requirement. Check the paper supply, displayed errors, connection, selected printer, and queue. If pages are blank, print a nozzle check to investigate ink delivery.

Why does my Canon printer show offline?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The computer may not be able to reach the printer, or it may be using an outdated entry. Check the USB or network connection and any available offline setting.

Should I reinstall the driver immediately?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Start with the printer display, test page, connection, and queue. Reinstallation is more useful when the printer itself works but the computer cannot print correctly.

Why does my printer print a test page but not my document?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The application, file, or its print settings may be responsible. Try another document and application, then compare the results.

Should I factory reset the printer?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A factory reset is usually unnecessary for initial troubleshooting. It can erase saved settings and require setup again.

Check Your Results
------------------

After each change, print one small document. Check that the job leaves the queue and that the page contains the expected text and colours.

If your Canon printer still won't print, note whether it can print a built-in test page and whether other devices can print. These details help narrow down the remaining cause.
